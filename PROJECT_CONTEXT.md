## 4. Tone of Voice & Content Guidelines

- **Technical Authority**: Clear, precise engineering language. Avoid fluff, buzzword soup, or marketing hype.
- **Restrained Confidence**: Demonstrate capability through structure, clarity, and visual execution rather than loud claims.
- **Strict Placeholder Rule**: Under no circumstances should fake testimonials, fictional client logos, artificial case study metrics, or fake office addresses be introduced into any layout.
